Philosophy

A personal philosophical framework exploring life, mortality, adaptation, strength, identity and the pursuit of personal perfection.

“Life can be understood as a journey toward an ideal that can never be completely reached. The value of the journey lies in how closely we approach that ideal.”

Adaptation is equally fundamental. Like water, an individual must be capable of changing form without losing essential identity.
