PART I — THE MEANING OF LIFE

We are all swimmers in the sea of life, looking toward the sun.

Every one of us carries the certainty that if we keep swimming, one day we will reach the sun — the sun being perfection.

But eventually the distance between us and the sun becomes fixed.

Our arms become exhausted and eventually we drown.

In both cases, death is the ending. And this is where the meaning of life begins.

The only one capable of conquering life and reaching the sun is the Creator of life Himself — God.

PART II — BE WATER

You must learn to adapt. Be like water.

Water changes its shape from one container to another without losing what it fundamentally is.

Water gives life when you drink it. But if you underestimate its power, it can drown you.

Waves represent movement. The tsunami represents overwhelming power. The lizard represents adaptation. The sun represents perfection. The sea represents life.
