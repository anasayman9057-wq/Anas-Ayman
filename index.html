THE SUN

PERFECTION / IDEAL

We are all, my friend, simply swimmers in the sea of life, looking toward the sun. Every one of us carries the certainty that if we keep swimming, one day we will reach the sun — the sun being perfection.


But the truth is that the distance between us and perfection may never completely disappear. We continue swimming until our strength runs out. In both possibilities, death is the ending.


And this is where the meaning of life begins. The meaning of life is represented in the ending of life itself — death.


The only one capable of conquering life and reaching absolute perfection is the Creator of life Himself — God.


The second half of my philosophy is about adaptation. Be like water. Water changes its shape from one container to another without losing what it fundamentally is.


Be adaptable. Be deep. Know your value. Understand your strength.


Water gives life. Water can also overwhelm. Waves can become enormous. A tsunami can transform the entire landscape.


All of this is simply H₂O.


My philosophy combines self-worth, danger, strength, depth and the ability to adapt.


Influenced by Nietzsche, Sartre and Bruce Lee, I have combined these ideas into something personal: the philosophy of Anas Ayman.
