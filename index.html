04 — PHILOSOPHY

A personal philosophy.

Part I — The Meaning of Life

We are all, my friend, swimmers in the sea of life, looking toward the sun. Each one of us believes that if we continue swimming, one day we will reach the sun — perfection itself.

Yet the truth is that your destiny is ultimately contained between two possibilities: a shark may devour you, or you may continue swimming, convinced that you will eventually reach the sun.

The further you swim, the more you realize that you are not actually reaching it. You can see the sun getting closer, but eventually the distance between you and it becomes fixed.

Your arms become exhausted, and eventually you die because you can no longer swim. In both possibilities, death is the end.

And this, to me, is part of the meaning of life: the meaning of life is represented in the end of life itself — death.

Death carries you toward a realm greater than life itself. The only one capable of overcoming life and reaching the sun completely is the Creator of life Himself — God.

He alone possesses perfection. We are all dead within the sea of life. Our purpose is simply to reach the closest possible distance to perfection and idealism.

Part II — Water and the Lizard

The second part of my philosophy is that you must become like water or like a lizard.

A fish dies when it leaves the sea because it cannot adapt. A lizard, however, can change its color according to the surface on which it stands.

So be a lizard, not a fish.

At the same time, being like water is extremely important. Water changes its form from one container to another. It can be a bottle, a cooler or a cup. It does not say no to its environment.

At the same time, water gives life when you drink it. It can also drown you if you underestimate its value and enter its depths without respect.

When water becomes violent, a tsunami can strike with enormous force. It can overwhelm whatever stands against it.

All of this is H₂O.

My philosophy combines self-worth, danger, strength, depth and adaptability. I draw inspiration from different philosophical and cultural ideas and combine them into my own personal perspective.

“We are all swimmers in the sea of life. Our purpose is to reach as close to perfection as we can.”

My Perspective

This philosophy represents the perspective through which I choose to understand life. It is a framework built around meaning, adaptation, personal value, strength and the awareness of mortality.
