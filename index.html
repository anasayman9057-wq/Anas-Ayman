04 — Philosophy

Thoughts
Beyond The Surface.

Life, hope, change, struggle and the meaning we create along the way.

We are all, my friend, swimmers in the sea of life, looking toward the sun. The sun represents hope. The sun represents life. Without hope, we would not still be here. Life without hope is like a desert without water. But you must understand, my friend, that the sun does not rise every day. Every sunrise has a sunset. So make use of your sunrise before your sunset. Remember that no matter how long life seems, it is ultimately short. Everything on this earth is temporary. Nothing remains exactly as it is forever. Work for your life as if you will live forever, and work for your afterlife as if you may die tomorrow. Remember that this world is a test. The more you love something in this life, the greater your test through it may become. The more attached you become to something, the more deeply you may be tested through it. This world is not a place of final reward. It is a place of trial. And when life tests you, do not allow the difficulty to destroy your hope. Learn from it. Grow through it. Become stronger because of it. Sometimes the hardest chapters of our lives become the chapters that define who we become. So keep moving. Keep believing. Keep building. Your story is not finished yet.
